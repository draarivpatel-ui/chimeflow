# ChimeFlow | AWS Communication Developer Services Agentic AI Partner Hackathon

Name: ChimeFlow, an omnichannel triage, guardrail and failover workbench

Tagline: Voice fails. The message still lands.

Description:
ChimeFlow is the bench for teams building agentic conversations on Amazon Chime SDK, AWS End User Messaging and Amazon SES. The failover bench replays four fictional scenarios deterministically across voice, text and email. Break a channel with a timeout, an undeliverable result or a carrier outage and watch the retries, the failover edges, the blocked channels with their reasons, and the human bridge on a millisecond timeline.

The message inspector counts GSM-7 and UCS-2 units the way carriers do, including two-unit extension characters and segment boundaries, masks Luhn-verified card numbers, SSNs, phone numbers, emails and clinical tokens before a message leaves a step, checks an 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM recipient local time window, and prices ten thousand sends from rates you set.

The simulator runs a scenario as a live conversation. Press the keypad during a simulated Chime PSTN prompt and each key plays its real dual tone, a low and a high sine wave, while a pure state machine branches. Free text goes through a confidence gate and red flag phrases escalate to a human.

The export tab produces SpeakAndGetDigits, PlayAudioAndGetDigits, CallAndBridge, ModifyChimeMeetingAttendees, End User Messaging SendTextMessage, SES SendEmail and CreateSipMediaApplication requests, plus a Lambda scaffold, each checked against the required fields in the AWS documentation.

Honest limits. Latencies, costs and rates are editable planning fixtures, not measurements or AWS prices. The exported payloads have never been deployed or sent. The TCPA window is an engineering rule, not legal advice. Every organisation in the scenarios is fictional and there are no customers or telemetry.

Built with: vanilla JavaScript, SVG, Web Audio API, node:test. No dependencies, no network calls.
