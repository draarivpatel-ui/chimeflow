          What this is, and what it is not

          • Planning model, not a measurement. Latencies, costs and the response delay are editable fixtures. They show how a flow behaves, not what your carriers will do.
          • Templates, not deployments. The exporters follow the request shapes in the AWS documentation. They have not been sent to a live account.
          • Policy gate, not legal advice. The 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM check is a conservative engineering rule. Which rules bind your messages depends on who you are texting and why.
          • Fictional organisations. The clinic, utility, airline and credit union are made up. There are no customers and no telemetry.
          • Redaction is pattern based. It catches the formats it knows, such as Luhn-valid card numbers and checksummed routing numbers. It will miss a name written in a sentence.

          Every organisation here is fictional. Every number is yours to edit.
